odin's Favourite Lasagna

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef in a skillet until browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Boil the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  5. In a baking dish, layer the noodles, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with cheese on top.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy!
