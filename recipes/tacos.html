odin's Favourite Tacos

Ingredients

  • Taco shells
  • Ground beef
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Diced tomatoes
  • Shredded cheese
  • Sour cream

Instructions

  1. Cook the ground beef in a skillet until browned.
  2. Warm the taco shells according to package instructions.
  3. Fill each taco shell with cooked ground beef.
  4. Add shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, and shredded cheese on top of the beef.
  5. Finish with a dollop of sour cream.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
